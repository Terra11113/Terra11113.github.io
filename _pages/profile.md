Research Preparation
======

My completed research focuses on automatic alignment and image-quality optimization for freeform ultra-short-throw projection systems. I have worked on optical simulation, structured image acquisition, geometric-distortion and MTF-related image-quality evaluation, C++ optimization, and closed-loop experimental validation.

I am now extending this measurement-and-correction discipline to real robot manipulation. I have built an SO-101 leader-follower platform with fixed external and wrist RGB cameras, collected a 60-episode dual-view language-conditioned manipulation dataset, established an ACT baseline, and fine-tuned and deployed SmolVLA for preliminary real-robot rollout evaluation. This platform is a controlled preparation for studying failure analysis, visual observability, and data-efficient human correction; it is not presented as a completed general-purpose VLA result.

More broadly, I am interested in research problems where physical systems and visual algorithms interact. My aim is to develop methods that combine measurement, learning-based perception, bounded action, and repeatable experimental verification.
