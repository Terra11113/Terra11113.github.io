## Project Overview

This project studies wavefront-aberration-guided automatic alignment of optical lens groups. Compared with MTF/PSF feedback alone, wavefront information can provide richer cues for estimating misalignment under certain optical configurations. The work combines optical simulation, software automation, and experimental feedback.

**Role:** Optical alignment software and experiment  
**Period:** 2025.5 - Present  
**Keywords:** wavefront aberration, Zernike coefficients, optical alignment, Zemax ZOS-API, Qt/C++, phase deflectometry, simulation-experiment comparison

## Technical Focus

- Built and tested mappings from low-order Zernike coefficients to lens-group misalignment parameters.
- Developed Qt/C++ modules based on ZOS-API to automate Zemax modeling, element perturbation, ray tracing, image-quality analysis, parameter scanning, and result export.
- Supported phase-deflectometry experiments for off-axis optical components and systems.
- Participated in acquisition workflow debugging, data processing, simulation-experiment comparison, and error analysis.

## My Role

I focused on software-assisted optical alignment, including Zemax automation, data export, interface-based workflow organization, and comparison between simulation and experimental results. This project also strengthened my experience in turning optical-alignment procedures into repeatable engineering software.

## Outcome

This project strengthened my preparation in optical-system automation, wavefront-based image-quality analysis, and software-supported optical alignment. In tested cases, RMS deviation from a Zygo interferometer was below 10 nm.
